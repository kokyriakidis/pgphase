# Remaining-gap diagnostic trial

Completed 114 cases with a bridge requiring at least two consistent clean SNPs per flank. 80 joins, 30 splits, four endpoint-unphased. Compared with multi_final: one added correct join at 24.36 Mb, no increased read discordance or switch/flips, no original-block majority reversals.

The comparison baseline is chr20_multi_final. These are overlapping local
windows; they do not establish whole-chromosome accuracy or NGC50.
