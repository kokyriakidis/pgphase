# Remaining-gap diagnostic trial

Experimental reassessment of provisional homozygous MSA indels, with phase-scoped output scoring. Four cases retain baseline target outcomes and read errors. At 12.74 Mb the deletion recovers 22 reference / 39 alternate observations, but its haplotype consensuses still collapse and the target remains split.

The comparison baseline is chr20_multi_final. These are overlapping local
windows; they do not establish whole-chromosome accuracy or NGC50.
