# Remaining-gap diagnostic trial

Experimental final read scoring within the reported PS, on top of the two-SNP block bridge. Four cases: 1.9 Mb discordance improves 30 to 23 but recovery still leaves the endpoints unphased; 26.6 Mb increases 34 to 35. This is not a validated endpoint repair and is excluded from the isolated clean-bridge implementation.

The comparison baseline is chr20_multi_final. These are overlapping local
windows; they do not establish whole-chromosome accuracy or NGC50.
